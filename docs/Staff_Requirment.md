#Staff Requirements
##1. Functional Requirements
###1.1 Account Management
|ID	     |Requirement
|--------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|STF-01	 |The system shall allow registered Staff members to log in using their email and password.
|STF-02	 |The system shall allow a logged-in Staff member to log out.
|STF-03	 |The system shall allow Staff members to change their account password.
|STF-04	 |The system shall display the Staff member's basic account information.
|STF-05	 |The system shall allow Staff members to update their phone number and other editable personal information.
|STF-06	 |The system shall prevent unauthorized users from accessing Staff accounts.

###1.2 Task Management
|ID	     |Requirement
|--------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|STF-07	 | The system shall allow Staff members to view the tasks assigned to them.                                     |
|STF-08	 |The system shall display the details of each assigned task, including its description and related event.      |
|STF-09	 |The system shall allow Staff members to mark their assigned tasks as completed after finishing the work.      |
|STF-10	 |The system shall display the status of each assigned task (pending, in progress and completed).               |
|STF-11	 |The system shall allow Staff members to view the deadline or expected completion time of their assigned tasks.|
|STF-12	 |The system shall notify Staff members when a new task is assigned to them.                                    |
|STF-13	 |The system shall allow Staff members to view their previously completed tasks.                                |
|STF-14	 |The system shall allow Staff members to report a task that they cannot complete due to a problem.             |

###1.3 Event Schedule
|ID	     |Requirement
|--------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|STF-15	 |The system shall allow Staff members to view the event schedule.                                              |
|STF-16	 |The system shall display event details, including the event date, time and venue location.                    |
|STF-17	 |The system shall allow Staff members to check their assigned tasks based on the event schedule.               |
|STF-18	 |The system shall display the starting and ending times of each scheduled event.                               |
|STF-19	 |The system shall notify Staff members when the event schedule changes.                                        |
|STF-20	 |The system shall allow Staff members to view upcoming events they are assigned to work on.                    |
|STF-21	 |The system shall display important instructions related to each event.                                        |

###1.4 Venue Issue Reporting
|ID	     |Requirement
|--------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|STF-22	 |The system shall allow Staff members to report any issue they find at the venue during event preparation or   |
while the event is taking place.                                                                                        |
|STF-23	 |The system shall allow Staff members to provide a description of the reported venue issue.                    |
|STF-24	 |The system shall allow Staff members to select the type of issue they want to report.                         |
|STF-25	 |The system shall allow Staff members to attach photos of the reported venue issue where necessary.            |
|STF-26	 |The system shall send reported venue issues to the Venue Manager for review and necessary action.             |
|STF-27	 |The system shall allow Staff members to view the status of their reported issues.                             |
|STF-28	 |The system shall notify Staff members when the Venue Manager responds to their reported issues.               |

###1.5 Status and Notifications
|ID	     |Requirement
|--------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|STF-29	 |The system shall allow Staff members to view notifications related to their assigned tasks and events.        |
|STF-30	 |The system shall notify Staff members when their task status changes.                                         |
|STF-31	 |The system shall notify Staff members about important event updates and schedule changes.                     |
|STF-32	 |The system shall allow Staff members to view their task completion history.                                   |
|STF-33	 |The system shall notify Staff members when the Venue Manager sends instructions or updates related to 
their work.                                                                                                             |

###1.6 Access Control
|ID	     |Requirement
|--------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|STF-34	 |The system shall not allow Staff members to view other users' private information.                            |
|STF-35	 |The system shall restrict Staff members from accessing Client, Venue Manager and Administrator functions.     |
|STF-36	 |The system shall allow Staff members to access only the tasks and event information assigned to them.         |
|STF-37	 |The system shall prevent Staff members from changing booking details, payment records and venue information.  |
